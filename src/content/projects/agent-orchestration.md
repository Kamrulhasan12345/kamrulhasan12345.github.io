An exploration of multi-agent orchestration patterns, implemented from scratch
rather than pulled from a framework:

- **Supervisor/Worker** delegation;
- **Pipeline/DAG** execution;
- **Debate-style** consensus between agents.

Building these by hand made the tradeoffs between the patterns concrete —
where coordination overhead pays off and where it just adds latency.
