# Evidence behind the thresholds

Cite these IDs in findings. "weak" means practitioner opinion, not a study. Re-check the URLs if a report depends on one.

| ID | Claim used in checks | Strength | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Visitors decide whether to stay in about 10 s; they read 20–28% of words | strong | NN/g, How Little Do Users Read? https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-little-do-users-read/ · How Long Do Users Stay on Web Pages? https://www.nngroup.com/articles/how-long-do-users-stay-on-web-pages/ |
| E2 | 57% of viewing time above the fold, 74% in the first two screens | strong | NN/g, Scrolling and Attention https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-attention/ |
| E3 | People scan headings (layer cake); put the key point first | strong | NN/g, F-Shaped Pattern https://www.nngroup.com/articles/f-shaped-pattern-reading-web-content/ |
| E4 | Recruiter résumé screen: 7.4 s (Ladders); 30–60 s (ResumeGo survey) | mixed | https://www.hrdive.com/news/eye-tracking-study-shows-recruiters-look-at-resumes-for-7-seconds/ · https://resumego.net/research/how-much-time-spend-resume |
| E5 | Visible nav works at about 4–5 top-level items; "7±2" is a myth for menus | strong | NN/g mobile nav https://www.nngroup.com/articles/find-navigation-mobile-even-hamburger/ · Dr. Dobb's https://drdobbs.com/web-development/the-myth-of-seven-plus-or-minus-2/184412300 |
| E6 | Plain labels carry information scent; clever names don't | moderate | NN/g https://www.nngroup.com/articles/fixing-bad-intranet-navigation/ |

No evidence either way: skill bars, testimonials, photos, open-to-work badges, PDF vs HTML résumé, contact form vs email. Don't add them because other portfolios have them.
